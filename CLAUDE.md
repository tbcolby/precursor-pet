# Tamagotchi — Build Notes
## What This Is
Virtual pet with lifecycle simulation for Precursor.
## Key Decisions
- 6 lifecycle stages with age-based evolution
- 5 stats with tick-based decay and action recovery
- ASCII art sprites per lifecycle stage
- Poop accumulation and cleaning mechanic
- Sickness from neglect, death from sustained neglect
- 3-second tick timer for real-time simulation
- 3 states: Naming, Status, Action
